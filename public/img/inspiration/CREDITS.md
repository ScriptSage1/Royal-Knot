# Photo credits — "Celebrations we love"

These photographs are **openly licensed images by independent photographers**, used as wedding inspiration. They are **not** photographs of Royal Knot client weddings, and the stories on the site are ideas inspired by them, not accounts of the people pictured.

All images were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons through its public API, then resized and compressed for the web. Metadata was removed during compression. No other changes were made. Resized copies of CC BY-SA images are shared under the same licence.
